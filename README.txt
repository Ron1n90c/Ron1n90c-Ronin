REQUIRES: GSE.

INSTALL
 1. Install GSE, then copy the "Ronin" folder to Interface\AddOns (both games use the same folder).
 2. If you had the old "RoninInstaller" or "RoninForever" folders, delete them first (Ronin replaces both).
 3. Log in, type /ronin. On the welcome screen choose your world: Forever or Retail.
 4. Choose Tank or DPS, tick the classes you want and press "Import Selected". Each row shows:
    Up to date / Update available / Not installed. Keep "Overwrite" ON to update.
 5. Open GSE, bind the sequence, put the macro on your action bar. After every GSE update, place the macro
    on the bar again.

COMMANDS
 /ronin            open (welcome screen)
 /ronin retail     open the Retail installer
 /ronin forever    open the Forever installer
 /ronin debug      print what the add-on sees in GSE (send me this if a status looks wrong)

 TANK - 12 sequences: Protection Warrior (Mountain Thane, Colossus), Protection Paladin (Lightsmith, Templar),
 Guardian Druid (Elune's Chosen, Druid of the Claw), Brewmaster Monk (Master of Harmony, Shado-Pan),
 Vengeance Demon Hunter (Aldrachi Reaver, Annihilator), Blood Death Knight (Deathbringer, San'layn).
 DPS - Unholy and Frost DK, Havoc DH, Retribution Paladin, Fury and Arms Warrior, Windwalker Monk,
 Feral and Balance Druid. One single-target macro and one AoE macro per hero tree (Windwalker: one macro).
 Versions inside each: Mythic+ / Mythic, Raid, Delve, Beginner (big defensives included) and LEVELING.
 LEVELING: only the basic spells you learn on the way to level 90, no hero talents needed. Pick it by hand
 in GSE (Versions) while you level, then switch back to Mythic+ / Raid at 90.
 Modifiers: Shift = heal / self-sustain, Alt = crowd control. Bind your interrupt on a separate key.

FOREVER V3.0.0 (WoW: Forever)
 TANK: Protection Paladin, Protection Warrior, Feral Druid (Bear).
 DPS: Retribution Paladin, Arms and Fury Warrior, Feral Druid (Cat), Enhancement and Elemental Shaman.
 Level ranges: 1-10, 10-40 and 40-60. Pick your range in GSE.
 Modifiers: Shift = heal, Ctrl = interrupt (Paladin: Hammer of Justice, Warrior: Shield Bash / Pummel),
 Alt = class extra (Consecration, Concussion Blow, Bash, totems).
 Spell IDs, so the macros work on any client language and new ranks never break them.

WHY I MAKE GSE MACROS
 I am a father, a working man and a person with some health problems I do not want to name. I made these
 macros so everyone, casual or hardcore, with a family or alone, can at least learn the rotation of every
 class and enjoy Azeroth to the fullest.
 How far can you push a Key+ with them? No macro in this world does everything for you. Without the
 mechanics and good positioning it will not work. Ronin takes care of the rotation, you take care of the rest.

RULES OF THE PACK
 No trinkets. Big defensives are in the Beginner and Leveling versions only (otherwise you press them).
 Every macro step stays under GSE's 255-character limit after compiling.
